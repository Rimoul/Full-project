About Seazonal Swaad

Seazonal Swaad is an online store dedicated to traditional Indian festival and puja-season foods.

We offer authentic, homemade-style delicacies that bring the taste of our rich culinary heritage to your home. Whether it's a festive celebration, a special occasion, or just a craving for something traditional — we're here to make it special for you.
